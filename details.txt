CH-BT: COAXIAL MWCNT/h-BN CORE-SHELL NANOWIRE INTERCONNECT MODEL SPECIFICATION
===============================================================================

1. THE SUB-2nm PHYSICAL BOTTLENECK
As conventional metallic interconnects (Copper/Cobalt/Ruthenium) scale down toward 
sub-2nm nodes, they hit a catastrophic thermal wall. Because the physical wire 
dimension is drastically smaller than Copper's intrinsic Mean Free Path (MFP ≈ 40nm), 
electrons suffer severe grain-boundary and surface roughness scattering. This elevates 
resistivity exponentially, causing devastating localized Joule heating.

2. CH-BT HETEROSTRUCTURE RESOLUTION
This repository models a nanoscale physical layout consisting of:
  * CONDUCTION CORE: An 8.4nm wide Multi-Walled Carbon Nanotube (MWCNT). CNTs possess 
    an extraordinarily large intrinsic MFP (extending into the micrometer scale), 
    allowing electrons to experience true ballistic transport without lattice collisions.
  * INSULATING SHELL: A 4.8nm thick Hexagonal Boron Nitride (h-BN) jacket wrap. h-BN 
    serves as an atomically smooth, isomorphic, wide-bandgap dielectric. This shell 
    eliminates dangling bonds, locks the wavefunctions within the core, and prevents 
    electron leakage while preserving the pristine atomic structure of the inner CNT.

3. TRANSPORT MATRIX
By engineering the channel layout such that the physical transport segment length (L) 
is far below the effective nanotube MFP (L << MFP_cnt), the system completely bypasses 
the classical Ohm's law regime. Conductance follows the quantum Landauer-Büttiker expression, 
effectively suppressing Joule heating across ultra-scaled logic nodes.
